How the Escrow Protocol Operates

Trustless cryptographic settlement backed by client-side encryption and Supabase Vaults.

STEP 01

Client-Side Lock & Checksums

Seller attaches multi-file archives. Files are hashed with SHA-256 and uploaded into private Supabase buckets with Row-Level Security.

STEP 02

On-Chain Transfer & Oracle Sync

Buyer enters the 6-digit room code, inspects asset sizes & checksums, sends crypto directly to the seller's wallet, and submits the TxHash.

STEP 03

Cryptographic Release & Receipt

Upon on-chain confirmation or seller approval, short-lived signed download links are generated and an official Escrow Certificate is issued.
